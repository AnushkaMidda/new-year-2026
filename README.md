# 🎉 New Year 2026 Website

**Live Demo:** [https://anushkamidda.github.io/new-year-2026/](https://anushkamidda.github.io/new-year-2026/)

---

## Overview

This is a **fun, interactive front-end project** designed to wish people a **Happy New Year 2026**. It is specifically targeted at the **Gen Z audience**, featuring:

- A vibrant, animated landing page with a warm New Year greeting.  
- A collection of **“unspoken confessions”** to reflect relatable thoughts and feelings.  
- A **user confession box** where visitors can type their own thoughts.  
  *(Note: submissions are temporary and reset on page refresh — no database or storage is used.)*

The project is purely **front-end** (HTML, CSS, and JavaScript) and is made for **fun and learning purposes**. It helped me revise and practice my **web development skills**.

## How to Use

1. Open [the live site](https://anushkamidda.github.io/new-year-2026/) in your browser.  
2. Explore the landing page greeting for 2026.  
3. Check the **“What Remains Unspoken”** section to read the preset confessions.  
4. Use the **confession box** to type your own thoughts (will be cleared on refresh).
